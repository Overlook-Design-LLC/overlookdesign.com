Delete your Minder account

  1. Sign into the Minder app on your phone
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu in the top-right corner
  3. Tap Delete Account
  4. Confirm the deletion
  5. You will be signed out of Minder
  6. All your data will be immediately and permanently deleted
