Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 11, 2026

Overlook Design ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Minder mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: When you register, we collect your email address to authenticate you.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Securely authenticate you to access Minder

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may delete your account and all associated data at any time directly within the App.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

Minder is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete your data. You can delete your account and all data directly in the App, or contact us at the address below.
