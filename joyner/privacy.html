Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 11, 2026

Overlook Design ("we," "our," or "us") operates the Joyner mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share information when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, and phone number.
  • Usage data: Information about how you interact with the App, including tee times created, RSVPs, and group membership.
  • Device information: Device type, operating system version, and unique device identifiers.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Securely authenticate you to access Joyner
  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App
  • Enable you to schedule and manage tee times and golf groups
  • Send you notifications related to your tee times and RSVPs
  • Communicate with you about updates and support
  • Monitor and analyze usage trends to improve the experience

3. Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information in the following circumstances:

  • With other users: Your name and profile information are visible to members of your golf groups and tee times you join.
  • Service providers: We use third-party services (such as Supabase for database hosting) that process data on our behalf under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal requirements: We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of our users.

4. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide services. You may request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us at the email below.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

Joyner is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have collected such information, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or delete your data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Third-Party Services

The App may contain links to third-party websites or services. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties and encourage you to review their privacy policies.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy in the App or by email. Your continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
